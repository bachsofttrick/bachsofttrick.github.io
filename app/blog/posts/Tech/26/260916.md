---
title: How to actually disable F11 global fullscreen on Lubuntu?
publishedAt: 2026-09-16
order: 1
hidden: false
---
1. Open your file manager, go to `/home/<username>/.config/openbox` and open `rc.xml` with a text editor.
2. Find the line, or tag with `F11` in the value. For example:

```
<keybind key="F11">
  <action name="ToggleFullscreen"/>
</keybind>
```

3. Comment out `<action name="ToggleFullscreen"/>`.
4. Log out or restart the PC.
