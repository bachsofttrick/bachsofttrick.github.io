After the hackathon from Cerebras, I have come to term with the fact that I suck at vibecoding complex piece of software. Burning through milions of tokens, and the app was unusable. I even make spec, plan files and everything, using GLM 5.3, which is a fairly powerful model to work on this. In the end, it didn't work as inteded at all. The code looked nice at first glance but I had no idea what went on inside. If I didn't rush this, taking things one piece at a time, maybe it will come out right. But it would still take more than 24 hours.

The only takeaway from this is my self-improving repo theory worked. Any changes to the codebase, any research, back-and-forth between me, my friend Yash Sharma and our coding agents, they all got written down in AGENTS.md and agent-docs. I used this theory and built a new repo called job-agent (the link below is the ready made version to be forked). As the agent works with you in applying for job through a connected browser (MCP or CLI, doesn't matter), it will keep notes on various trials, tribulation, your profile, preferences, etc. AGENTS.md and various subfiles will keep being updated, further imparting knowledge to the agent (assuming it does re-read those files).
